About this Course

Speculative design uses design to ask questions rather than solve problems. In this course we imagine possible futures shaped by emerging technologies, build provocative artifacts and scenarios, and use them to open conversations about what futures we want.

New studio tool: Speculative Kano Analysis

Use the Speculative Kano Analysis to test the features of your 2045 object with classmates playing stakeholder roles. Look for features that the powerful want but the vulnerable reject: that is where your provocation lives. Studio Tools

Speculative House of Quality

List the stakeholders in your 2045 world and what they need, then score how strongly each feature of your object serves them. The house shows which features the future will build, and who it leaves out.

Interactive worksheet · Export as PNG, PDF, or CSV · Save and reopen your work
Open ↗

Speculative Kano Analysis

Classmates play stakeholder roles and say how they feel with and without each feature of your object. The analysis sorts features into Must-be, Performance, Attractive, Indifferent or Reverse, and flags features one group wants that another rejects.
