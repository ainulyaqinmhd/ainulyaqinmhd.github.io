Speculative House of Quality

List who lives in your 2045 world and what they need, then score how strongly each feature of your object serves them. The house shows which features the future will build, and who it leaves out.

    Your reading

    How to fill it in

    1. Rows: write one need for each stakeholder, including someone vulnerable and the AI system itself.
    2. Importance: score 1–5 the way the 2045 world would, by how much power they hold.
    3. Columns: name 3–6 features of your object.
    4. Matrix: how strongly does each feature serve each need?
    5. Roof: mark features that support or work against each other.
    6. Read it: who wins, who gets nothing, and which feature carries your provocation?

    Key

    ●9 strong: the feature is built for this need ○3 medium △1 weak ·0 none ++ / +Roof: the two features strengthen each other −− / −Roof: the two features work against each other

    Feature score = Σ (importance × relationship). The “Served?” column adds up relationships only, so it shows how much the design does for each person regardless of their power.
