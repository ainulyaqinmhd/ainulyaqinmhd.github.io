SIF Lab · 智慧互動未來實驗室

Human–AI Interaction · Interaction Design · HCI

We study how people interact with intelligent and immersive technologies, and design systems that make learning, creativity, and everyday life better. Based in the Department of Information Communication, Yuan Ze University, Taoyuan, Taiwan.

Meet the Team Join the Lab

What We Do

Research Focus

Human-AI Interaction & AIGC

Designing and evaluating AI-supported tools, from NLP and RAG agents to visual language models, with people kept in the loop.

UX Research & Assessment

Methods for measuring user experience, combining surveys, behavioral data, and decision-making models.

Immersive & Wearable Technology

VR experiences and wearable devices that support collaboration, learning, and wellbeing.

Design & Technology Education

Scaffolding computational thinking and design skills in HCI education and STEAM learning.
