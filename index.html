Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Communication at Yuan Ze University, with a Ph.D. in Design from National Taipei University of Technology.

I work across HCI, UX research and assessment, and AIGC applications (NLP, RAG, VLM) for design innovation and education, turning research into working systems, from AI agents to immersive VR learning tools.

I teach Wearable Device Technologies and Speculative Design, with more than five years of English-taught teaching and thesis mentoring, and publications in IEEE Transactions on Education and Interactive Learning Environments.
