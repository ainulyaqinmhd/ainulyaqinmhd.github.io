In my role as an assistant for the Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Design course, I have been actively involved in introducing students to the fundamental concepts and practices of HCI design. The course is structured to provide a comprehensive understanding of the user interface design process and its applications, equipping students with the skills necessary for practical interface design. Key topics covered include conceptual models, interface usability, the HCI process, user requirements, interface prototyping, principles of interface design, and user testing. Instruction is delivered through a combination of lectures, case studies, and hands-on practical work, fostering an engaging learning environment. Student evaluation is based on reports, oral presentations, and design projects, ensuring that learners can effectively apply their knowledge and demonstrate their understanding of HCI principles in real-world contexts. This course aims to prepare students to create user-centered designs that enhance the interaction between humans and computers.
