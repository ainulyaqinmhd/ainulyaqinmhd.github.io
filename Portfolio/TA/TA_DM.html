Design Method - Prototyping for Designer

Project information

  • Category: University
  • Project session: Spring 2022
  • Project URL: Modules

"Design Method - Prototyping for Designers" course, which equips participants with essential skills in using Arduino as a versatile prototyping tool. The course begins with an introduction to Arduino, emphasizing its relevance in design projects, followed by a hands-on light control setup where students design a light-based prototype for a traffic light system. Participants then practice fundamental visualization techniques by displaying information on a monitor, enhancing their ability to communicate design ideas effectively. The course culminates in a project integration phase, where students develop a comprehensive light control system that incorporates a buzzer and integrates a distance sensor, reinforcing the skills learned and highlighting the importance of combining multiple components to create functional and innovative design solutions. Overall, the course provides a robust foundation in prototyping methods, preparing designers to tackle real-world challenges with confidence and creativity.
