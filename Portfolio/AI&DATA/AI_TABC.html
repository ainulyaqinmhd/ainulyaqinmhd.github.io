Venture Your Green Investment Capital: AI REITs Platform

Project information

  • Category: LLM, GIS, AIGC
  • Collaboration: TABC
  • Project URL: Website | RAG Chatbot

This project aims to revolutionize real estate investment and sustainability practices by integrating advanced features into a unified platform. Key components include an AI-powered chatbot for real-time inquiries on sustainable real estate, a GIS-based tool to visualize geographical risks like climate change, and engaging AI-generated educational videos for YouTube. The platform leverages GRESB certification, a globally recognized ESG benchmark, to link sustainability performance with financial outcomes, supporting strategic decision-making in green finance. By collaborating with GRESB Asia, the project promotes certification in Taiwan, enhancing international ESG standards and fostering green financing pathways, while demonstrating Taiwan's commitment to innovative asset management and climate action.
