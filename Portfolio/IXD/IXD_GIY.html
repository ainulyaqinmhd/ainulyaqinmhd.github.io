Gravity is Your Enemy!

Project information

  • Category: Game Design, Unity
  • Project URL: Report

In this game, players find themselves lost in space, battling against the forces of gravity created by unknown scenarios. The primary obstacles are represented by meteors, and players must skillfully navigate their spaceship to avoid collisions. The game emphasizes a precision-based experience, encouraging players to feel skillful rather than frantic, with a focus on careful control rather than button-mashing.

The core mechanic revolves around skillfully flying the spaceship and avoiding environmental hazards. Players progress through levels by moving from a starting point to a finish line, with each completed level unlocking the next. As players advance, the game environment becomes darker and introduces more obstacles, requiring them to adapt their movements.

Utilizing the concept of onion design, the most critical feature of the game is player movement, which is essential for gameplay. Gravity plays a significant role, as players must control their thrust to maintain their trajectory. Collision with the environment adds stakes, as players face consequences for their actions, while level progression keeps the gameplay engaging and dynamic. The game screen will also include elements such as the background story, success and failure indicators, and cheat codes, enhancing the overall player experience.
