Virtual Pet Embodiment with Machine Learning Approaches

Project information

  • Category: App design
  • Project URL: Report

This project aims to address modern challenges related to pet ownership, particularly for individuals in apartments or college students who lack the space or resources for a real pet. By leveraging machine learning and augmented reality, the app provides an interactive virtual pet that guides users in proper pet care, relieves stress, and enhances happiness through direct feedback from their virtual companion. The app encourages a more active lifestyle by integrating features such as health monitoring, voice communication, and haptic feedback. Users can engage in activities like feeding, grooming, and playing with their virtual pet, while also receiving notifications and weekly health targets to promote responsibility and well-being. This cost-effective solution offers a fun and engaging way to experience pet ownership without the associated challenges of real pets.
